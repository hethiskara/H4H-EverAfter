### Full Description (4000 characters max)

```
PRESERVE WHAT MATTERS, BEFORE TIME FADES

EverAfter is an AI-powered memory preservation app designed for everyone who wants to capture life's precious moments — whether you're supporting a loved one with memory loss, honoring someone who passed too soon, documenting your family's legacy, or simply journaling your personal journey.

🎙 VOICE MEMORIES
Capture stories in their own voice through natural, guided conversation. Our AI helps prompt meaningful questions and organizes memories automatically. Perfect for preserving loved ones' voices or your own reflections.

🧠 AI-POWERED RECALL
Ask questions about your past or a loved one's story, and watch memories come alive with context and emotion. Our AI understands the connections between memories and helps you rediscover forgotten moments.

📅 TIMELINE VISUALIZATION
See your entire life's journey beautifully organized across time. Emotion indicators, themes, and meaningful connections make it easy to explore your story chronologically or by feeling.

🌌 MEMORY NETWORK
Discover how memories connect and form the constellation of your life story. See patterns, themes, and the threads that weave your narrative together in an interactive visualization.

🔥 STREAK TRACKING
Build the habit of memory preservation with daily streak tracking. Stay motivated to capture moments regularly and watch your memory collection grow.

WHO IS EVERAFTER FOR?

• Families supporting loved ones with dementia or Alzheimer's
• Those honoring the memory of loved ones gone too soon
• Families documenting their legacy for future generations
• Anyone who wants a meaningful personal journal
• Caregivers helping preserve patient stories
• Genealogy enthusiasts building family archives

KEY FEATURES:

✓ Voice recording with AI-guided prompts
✓ Photo and text memory capture
✓ Conversational AI memory recall
✓ Beautiful timeline visualization
✓ Emotion tracking and analysis
✓ Theme and connection discovery
✓ Secure, private, encrypted storage
✓ Easy sharing with family members
✓ Offline mode available
✓ Export your memories anytime

PRIVACY & SECURITY:

Your memories are sacred. We use end-to-end encryption, offer local storage options, and never sell your data. Your stories remain yours, forever.

BUILT FOR HUMANITY:

EverAfter was created with a simple mission: preserve the human experience in all its forms. We believe memory preservation is a human right, not a luxury. That's why we're committed to keeping EverAfter accessible, private, and focused on what truly matters: the stories that make us human.

START PRESERVING TODAY:

Download EverAfter and begin capturing the stories that matter most. Whether for yourself, a loved one, or future generations — every memory preserved is a gift to the future.

Made with 🤍 for humanity.
```
